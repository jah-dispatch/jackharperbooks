About Jack Harper

Crafting Standalone MM Romance

Welcome! I’m Jack Harper, an independent author dedicated to writing raw, emotional, character-driven MM romance. My passion lives in standalone stories—capturing intense, slow-burn connections and high-stakes emotional dynamics without forcing characters into endless series constraints.

Whether exploring time-bending romantic longing, unexpected intimacy, or deep personal redemption, I focus on the heartbeat of two people finding their way to each other against impossible odds.

When I’m not at the keyboard crafting my next project, you can usually find me building digital tools, refining creative visual concepts, and spending quiet evenings planning the next great story.
